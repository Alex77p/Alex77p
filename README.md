## 👋 About Me

I'm **Alex sefer**, also known online as **Alex77p**. I build experimental and practical AI software, with a particular interest in **Android AI assistants, local/offline inference, GGUF models, automation, and autonomous software engineering**.

My main projects are **Axtor** and **MyAI**. Together they explore how AI can become more useful on personal devices while keeping computation and control as local as possible.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🤖 [Axtor — Android AI Assistant](https://github.com/Alex77p/Axtor)

Axtor is my Android AI assistant project, exploring local AI, voice interaction, custom GGUF models, and Android device automation.

**Focus:** Android AI · Offline/Local AI · GGUF · Llama · Voice · Automation

### 🧠 [MyAI — AI Core & Autonomous Software](https://github.com/Alex77p/MyAI)

MyAI is my broader AI-system project, exploring local inference, automated testing and repair, component lifecycle management, self-improvement planning, and autonomous software evolution.

**Focus:** AI systems · Local inference · Automation · Testing · Repair · Component evolution
